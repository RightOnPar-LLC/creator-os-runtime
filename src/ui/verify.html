Check a receipt

Every receipt on the Mesh is signed. Paste one here, or a receipt id, and see each check for yourself. The signature is checked twice: by our server, and in your own browser against the public key we publish, so you don't have to take our word for it.

The receipt

  

  
How it works: the receipt body is canonical JSON (keys sorted, no spaces), signed with Ed25519. Its prev field is the hash of the receipt before it, so history can't be edited without breaking the chain.
